Treatment Options

Find the Right Fit for Your Smile

Clear Braces

Made of ceramic material, clear braces are far less visible on your teeth than metal — and can still be paired with colored elastics.

Invisalign

A series of clear, removable aligners that gradually straighten your teeth. Snug at first, they become comfortable with wear.

Metal Braces

The most common and time-tested option, today's high-grade stainless steel braces are more comfortable than ever.

Airway Orthodontics

Snoring, mouth breathing, or restless sleep? Dr. Cartier partners with an onsite ENT for whole-health care beyond straight teeth.

Airway orthodontics looks at how jaw, teeth, and airway development impacts breathing and sleep. Snoring, mouth breathing, restless sleep, or trouble focusing at school can be signs.
