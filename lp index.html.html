Questions, answered

Can you customise the menu? Is it only Mexican food?

Mexican is what made our name, but it's not a limit. Every menu is built around your event — vegetarian options, kids' favourites, milder dishes, or items well outside the Mexican kitchen. Tell us what you'd like and we'll make it work.

What kinds of events do you cater?

All of them — birthdays, weddings, corporate lunches, graduations, quinceañeras, reunions, holiday parties, school and community events. If you're bringing people together, we'll handle the food.

How many people can you cater for?

From small family gatherings to large celebrations. Tell us your headcount in the form and we'll confirm straight away.

Do you provide servers and setup?

Yes — full-service means our team arrives, sets up, serves your guests, and cleans up after. You host; we work.

How far in advance should I book?

The earlier the better for weekend dates, but we do our best to accommodate short-notice events too — send the form and we'll tell you honestly if we can make your date work.
