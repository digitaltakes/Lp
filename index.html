Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every inspection and estimate is free, with no pressure to sign anything on the spot. We document the damage or scope with photos and give you an itemized estimate.

How fast can you respond to storm damage?

Our storm response team is available 24/7. For active leaks or wind and hail damage, we prioritize tarping and temporary repairs to stop further damage before we schedule the full fix.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold our own Florida licenses in general contracting (CBC1269628), roofing (CCC1331891), and mold assessment and remediation (MRSA5363, MRSR5423), and we carry general liability and workers' compensation on every job.

Do you work with insurance claims?

We provide detailed photo documentation and itemized estimates that you can submit to your insurance carrier. Ask your project manager for guidance on the claims process for your specific job.

What areas do you serve?

We serve homeowners and businesses across Central, North, and parts of Southwest Florida. See the full list of cities above, or call us to confirm coverage for your address.

What if I only need a small repair, not a full roof?

No job is too small. We handle single-shingle repairs and minor leaks the same way we handle full re-roofs: licensed work, honest pricing, and a warranty behind it.
