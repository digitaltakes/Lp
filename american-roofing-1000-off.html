Frequently Asked Questions

How do I claim the $1,000 off?

Request a quote with the form on this page or call 386-473-1174 and mention the offer before it ends on October 31, 2026. We schedule a free roof inspection, give you an itemized estimate, and take $1,000 off the contract price of your new roof. The offer covers both residential and commercial new roof installations and full replacements.

How much does a roof replacement cost?

Roof replacement cost depends on the size and pitch of the roof, the material you choose (asphalt shingle, metal or tile), and the condition of the decking underneath. That is why every quote starts with a free inspection. You get an itemized written estimate with the $1,000 discount shown, and no pressure to sign on the spot.

What types of roofs do you install?

For homes, we install asphalt shingle, metal and tile roofs. For commercial properties, we install TPO, EPDM, metal and modified bitumen roofing on flat and low slope roofs. Every installation is handled by manufacturer-certified crews.

Is a workmanship warranty included?

Yes. Every new roof installation includes our workmanship warranty, on top of the manufacturer's warranty on materials. Your written estimate lays out exactly what is covered.

Can you replace a commercial roof without shutting down my business?

In most cases, yes. We offer off-hours scheduling and plan the job around your operations to keep disruption to tenants and customers to a minimum.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every inspection and estimate is free, with no pressure to sign anything on the spot. We document the roof with photos and give you an itemized estimate.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold our own Florida licenses in roofing (CCC1331891) and general contracting (CBC1269628), along with mold assessment and remediation (MRSA5363, MRSR5423), and we carry general liability and workers' compensation on every job.

What areas do you serve?

We serve homeowners and businesses across Central and East Central Florida, including Seminole, Orange, Volusia, Brevard and Flagler counties and Sebastian, along with other parts of the state. See the full list of cities above, or call us to confirm coverage for your address.
